Two seasons, two kinds of holiday.

Winter

Ski weeks

The Tannenheim gondola station is about 50 metres from the house: boots on, skis on your shoulder, off you go. The Flumserberg ski area is usually open from early December until Easter, depending on snow.

Christmas, New Year and the February ski holidays go first. It pays to ask early.

Summer

Hiking weeks

In summer Flumserberg becomes hiking country, high above Lake Walen with the Churfirsten across the valley. Up to the alpine pastures in the morning, back at the long table in the evening.

We often use the flat ourselves in summer, so summer weeks are limited.

Frequently asked questions

How many people does the flat sleep?

Ideal for six: six beds in three bedrooms (two double beds and a bunk bed). If needed, two more people can sleep on the sofa bed.

How far is the Flumserberg ski area?

The Flumserberg Tannenheim gondola station is about 50 metres from the house in a straight line. The ski season usually runs from early December until Easter, depending on snow.

How do I get there by public transport?

Take the train to Flums, then the direct bus from Flums station to the stop “Flumserberg, Tannenheim” (about 18 minutes). The house is about 130 metres from the stop.

Is there parking?

On request. We tell you where to park with our offer.

Is there Wi-Fi?

On request.

Are pets allowed?

On request. Please ask us before booking.

How is the flat let and what does it cost?

By the week; we agree the arrival and departure day with you. Prices on request. Tourist tax of approx. CHF 3.40 per person per night from age 6 is added.

What is in the kitchen?

Oven, hob with extractor hood, coffee machine and kettle. The dining table seats eight.

Can I rent the flat in summer?

Yes, but availability is limited: we often use the flat ourselves in summer. Ask for the week you have in mind.

Where exactly is the flat?

At Panoramastrasse 8, 8897 Flumserberg Tannenheim, municipality of Flums, canton of St. Gallen, Switzerland. The house sits above Lake Walen and faces the Churfirsten.
